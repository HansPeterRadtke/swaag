## Answers to the four explicit questions

**Numeric versus textual relevance.** There is no justified winner for installed models. Constrained enum and bounded integer are both machine-sortable and can express identical ordinal targets. A textual ordered enum might improve validity or semantic consistency; a numeric result might provide more resolution or avoid verbose labels. They must be compared on the *same* cases, dimensions and token/call costs, including abstention/invalid schema outcomes. The current fixture spans user-facing, worker-quality and orchestration relevance; labels are not probabilities. Its nine-level scale is a deliberately limited *test encoding*, not permanent relevance policy. A larger adjudicated, multi-annotator task-specific holdout is needed before drawing conclusions.

**Combined versus specialist calls, context, ordering and quantization.** No universal direction is established. Combined calls can preserve global context and latency; decomposition can isolate difficult tasks but may lose cross-stage information and multiply latency and cascading errors. Even longer-capacity models show positional retrieval problems. The primary *Lost in the Middle* experiment shows degraded performance for evidence placed in the middle, not an evaluated result on the present Qwen services (https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/). The existing SWAAG decomposition hypothesis register is the canonical starting baseline. Preregister fixed facts at input beginning/middle/end; relevant/irrelevant expansion and adversarial conflicting updates; fused versus separate extraction/selection/judgment; actual installed quantized endpoint identities; seeds, greedy and sampled regimes, schema, token counts, correctness, first token and full latency. Crucially, the October twentieth one-call initial user-reply requirement must not be violated for production experiments.

**Extraction/reasoning ensembles.** Repeated identical but incorrect answers are not independent truth. Evaluate first-pass extraction versus reasoning separately against exact human/source or formal oracles; then vary model, independently semantically equivalent prompts, order and seeds with correlated failures explicitly counted. The offline scorer flags unanimous wrong outputs as wrong, not as successful peer review. The two extraction fixtures exercise quotation and hypothetical claims; reasoning fixtures exercise unknown inference and arithmetic. No inference results were generated, so no ensemble improvement or error correlation is measured. Compare error correlation conditionally on independently verified outcomes and report additional cost.

**Syntax/grammar auxiliary training.** Published results are task- and method-dependent. Glavaš and Vulić (EACL 2021, https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.270/) report limited and inconsistent downstream language-understanding gain from intermediate parsing supervision on their evaluated transformer setup. Strubell et al. (EMNLP 2018, https://aclanthology.org/D18-1548/) report semantic-role-labeling gains from linguistically informed multi-task attention in their architecture. Neither demonstrates a universal win on modern installed Qwen LLMs. A correct test would train a matched architecture/tokenizer/corpus/splits with syntactic auxiliary labels versus equal-compute ordinary training and a shuffled-label negative control, with held-out language modeling and generalization tasks; no local training was attempted.

## Preregistered next experiment and gates

Before calling any Thor endpoint, obtain read-only, contemporaneous evidence from the model supervisor for user/worker queue and every serving slot, active SWAAG operations, service and endpoint identities, unified GPU memory headroom and disk state; if busy, do not issue test inference. Compare equivalent prompts/scales for identical inputs, randomized experiment order, fixed quantization, identical maximum output budget and explicit schema; repeat at least three seeds in both greedy and mild-sampling regimes. Never run a locally retrained model on Thor without separate resource authorization. Preserve complete model outputs and timestamps only in private experiment storage, avoiding source credentials and personal content. Add independent ground-truth adjudication and heldout examples before trial, and use a new untouched holdout if selecting a winner on these fixtures.
