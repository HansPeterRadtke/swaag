# Corrective relevance benchmark protocol — preregistered before Qwen calls

**Owner question, not a software-test-count task:** what information is necessary to do real agent work versus to tell the user, and do installed LLMs judge those dimensions more reliably as ordered text labels or numeric levels? This is a *developmental role-relevance study*, not a general validation or an independent human-rated corpus. Original prior toy/malformed-output experiments are preserved separately and are not pooled here.

## Source, authenticity, context, annotation scope

The source documents are **genuine SWAAG engineering investigation reports of earlier real Qwen model calls**, not invented hypothetical approval ledgers. They cover actual role routing, failed attempts to use model identifiers as routing keys, stale delivered worker progress, end-to-end-vs-synthetic test qualification, the measured multi-minute foreground bottleneck, and fast speech handoff. They are **original agent-generated report paragraphs**, not unmodified direct user utterances or full raw inference logs. Every candidate's exact paragraph and character offsets plus SHA-256 of its containing document are preserved in `authentic_relevance_v1.json`. The exact original requirements and full source files remain in their canonical locations; the benchmark excludes external private credentials and personal data.

Four task/role contexts, derived from genuine owner requirements and archived SWAAG status reports: user-facing voice readiness report, background worker debugging a rejected planning request, orchestrator tracking running/completed workers with GPU prioritization, and user-facing software/model identity question. Each case contains exact role, original task purpose, surrounding project/operational context, and one source-exact candidate paragraph. The document-length background is not reproduced in every model call; the relevant role/project goal and source paragraph are. The construction has 64 labeled role/passage examples, 32 marked indispensable and 32 marked not needed for that *particular* immediate role/purpose. Importantly, these **extreme-level annotations are made by the research author interpreting the already written user/project requirements**, *not* separately blinded human adjudication. They are reproducible task-contract anchors; no claim of external inter-rater reliability or representative natural-world sampling is made. Some source facts could be worth keeping for reasons beyond the narrow role and therefore disagreement need not mean a model is objectively wrong.

The paragraph constructor initially grouped adjacent Markdown list bullets into one candidate and thus risked contradictory labels. This was identified and corrected **before freezing the dataset and before any model requests** by splitting list-item source spans while retaining exact offsets. No experimental errors were removed or rescored.

## Predeclared comparison

The frozen `run_authentic_relevance.py` selects 32 instances: the first four required and first four omit examples from **each** of the four roles, then applies a fixed deterministic shuffle. Each case is scheduled for the two pre-installed Thor endpoints (Qwen3-Coder Q4, Qwen3.8 Q8), in each of the two constrained outputs (integer 0–8 and the matching nine-level textual enum). Exact textual definitions and role/passage input are identical; only field output type and instruction differs. Per-case run order is deterministically shuffled to avoid always privileging one model or one encoding. Temperature zero, seed 17, top-p one, 50-token output cap, generation-time schema decoding and thinking disabled. The full planned comparison has 128 short independent calls but is only undertaken when each request passes the original read-only SWAAG supervisor-and-native-slot admission check; each invocation permits at most twenty calls. No model starts, downloads, restarts, preemption, external effects, storage-intensive work or changes to production workers.

**Primary measurements**: structural validity; exact agreement with the explicit extreme developer-provided anchor (0 or 8); thresholded required/not-required classification (output >=6 counts required); mean absolute ordinal distance from anchor; paired numeric/category disagreements for identical model/case/seed; full wall latency, cached/prompt/completion tokens; role swaps where exactly the same source fact should be important for a worker but not for a user-facing reply. These labels do not measure a calibrated percentage probability. A fair *downstream* check would compare complete final user answers/worker effects with the omitted/retained passages; **this study cannot infer downstream correctness merely from isolated labels**. If prompts, boundaries, dataset selection or labeling are revised after looking at these results, these data become development data and a fresh untouched external holdout is required before general selection.

**No inference from unrelated quantizations:** Coder Q4 and Qwen3.8 Q8 are *different* base models as well as precision regimes; their comparison may show local task differences, but never isolates quantization. If the multi-user GPU becomes busy, the missing cells remain genuinely missing, not failures attributed to either model. The existing one-initial-user-facing-model-call production contract is untouched by the research.

## Reproduction and separate evidence

`authentic_relevance_v1.py` regenerates/checks source-derived entries; `test_authentic_relevance.py` checks source digests, role label balances and case-id uniqueness. `run_authentic_relevance.py` executes only bounded idle-admitted model calls; every request is durably recorded before dispatch with full role, goal, source, JSON schema and admission status, followed by returned output and usage. Unknown results are **never retried**. On Thor, run it against a new scratch-only journal path; transfer raw bytes back without editing. Evidence files must remain separate from the previous exploratory fixtures, raw successes, failed generation and capacity refusals. Inferential claims need independent blinded human relevance adjudications and representative genuine agent trajectories plus final answer/worker outcome evaluation. Until then all estimates are descriptive developer-annotation concordance, not independently verified semantic accuracy.
