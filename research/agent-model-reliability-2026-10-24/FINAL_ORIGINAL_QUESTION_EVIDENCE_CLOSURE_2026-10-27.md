## Direct answer to the original questions

**(A) Relevance for the user versus the worker versus orchestrator:** It is not a single global text-interest score. The user-facing model needs the specific current requested answer, source-backed status, important uncertainty and any original detail the owner explicitly requests; workers need the entire operative original task, relevant prohibitions, exact source/tool observations and accepted completion predicates; orchestration needs task IDs, dependencies, source versions, active worker revisions, model admission and persistent tool/effect receipts. A source can be essential to worker quality and irrelevant to ordinary spoken progress, or essential to speech when the owner requests internal details. Source identity, quotation/time/world scope and later explicit user correction must be retained. **This structural requirement is supported by the original recordings and software source. Automated selector correctness remains unproved.** The authentic 29-SWAAG-excerpt, 64-role-label corpus has eleven researcher-labelled source-context reversals, but no independent multi-rater or executable original end-task relevance gold. The older eight-case Qwen evidence selection corpus also **leaks all its relevant labels as `r1` and `r2` in every case**, allowing a zero-call source-ID heuristic to win an invalid blind relevance test. Do not promote these labels to source semantic truth.

**(B) Numeric versus ordered textual relevance labels:** Neither wins on existing locally measured source/matched model prompts. Five corresponding local Qwen same-task/source/role pairs matched the *single researcher's anchor* exactly on **3/5** numeric 0–8 scores and **3/5** mapped ordered words. Absolute ordinal errors were 10 numeric versus 16 words. A threshold-six necessary/not-necessary translation gave 4/5 numeric and 3/5 text, still too few and not independently adjudicated. Numeric or textual levels can be mapped to a sortable order but **are not calibrated truth probabilities**; maintain separate dimensions for user, worker and orchestration. No global score format change is justified from this evidence alone. The underlying Thor numeric-vs-category raw run was **not re-invoked** in this round and no denied operation was retried.

**(C) One user-facing call versus source selection, specialization and second-model review:** **Retain one capable initial user-facing semantic call as the current provisional choice**, followed by independently verifiable tools or independently necessary background worker calls when source/task evidence demands it. It is **not a universal proof that model decomposition can never improve correctness**. The original local Qwen Coder-vs-Qwen3.8 questions were very small and had different models and quantizations (five originally paired full-context task examples, Coder 4/5 and Qwen3.8 5/5). A separately available SHA-pinned Nitro archive has only **9 completed full direct answers** overall (Coder 4/5, Qwen3.8 4/4), **4 completed researcher-oracle-selected separate answers**, and **8 real model selector *first stages***, none with an executed stage-two final answer. These different archived populations must not be conflated into the same five-task claim.

In **six truly matched original case+model comparisons**, the **completed direct answers** cost **5,593 actual measured tokens and 20.874356 seconds**, with **5/6** agreement against the author's answer key. The **first selector stages alone** cost **5,600 actual measured tokens and 21.826964 seconds**, so even before adding the mandatory final answer, their aggregate excess was **+7 tokens and +0.952608 seconds**; five of six first selectors were individually slower than their direct full answers. The **maximum possible correctness improvement over the existing 5/6 author-key direct result on these same six tasks is one additional matched success**, if a future complete specialist run were always right and never overturned the other five. That is **a theoretical ceiling under nonindependent researcher answer labels**, not observed stage-two correctness. Two case/model pairs have a completed selector stage and an independently separate *researcher-selected* oracle-answer trial; one has the same selected source IDs, but **zero** has full direct+selector+oracle-answer all three and **none is an authentic model-selected serial completion**. Summing separate oracle trials to fabricate actual two-stage latency or claiming a stage-two reviewer accuracy from model votes would be scientifically false.

A genuine published external software-agent task study was independently reanalyzed on **20 distinct SWE-bench Verified and 20 Terminal-Bench task IDs**, repeated across seven exact model versions, with independent published final task grades. Observable no-output is a high-specificity but low-recall failure trigger: 14 of 77 primary failures and 19 of 102 respectively. Additional rerouted full architectures rescued some failures and **broke some original successes**; per-task GPUs, actual tokens, VRAM and production SWAAG effect receipts were unavailable. An independent SciFact human-expert claim/paper experiment found **103/209 cases where two different CPU lexical classifiers agreed and were both wrong**, and using the second as a reviewer corrected nine of the first's mistakes but introduced ten new ones. Those are genuine externally labelled negative controls **for their respective CPU/published domains**, not measured local Qwen reviewer precision. They justify *independent external output checking*, not bigger-model authority or votes.

**(D) Training-time grammar/syntax auxiliaries:** Published controlled work has observed syntax/perplexity benefits on particular data and model designs and regressions elsewhere. There is **no** original same-initial-weights, same-training-FLOP, same-corpus Qwen3-Coder/Qwen3.8 experiment with downstream SWAAG final-task correctness, so no local policy/training superiority claim is justified. Prompted grammar tags, small synthetic GRUs and constrained JSON formatting are not equivalent to an auxiliary loss during modern transformer training. Running large shared-GPU training in this context was neither safe nor authorized by a verified resource lease and was not attempted.

## What is closed and what remains an evidence gap

**Closed and defensible:** original role-specific relevance criteria and source authority rules; existence and failure modes of source-ID leakage; exact first-stage versus direct *measured cost lower bound*; independently reproduced task/result loss under consensus or unverified claims in bounded non-Qwen domains; original source-to-final tool-effect and restart identity under scripted model outputs; per-session JSONL race and source-branch fix. The original experiments and negative results were preserved; denied operations were not disguised/retried.

**Still unproved — exactly:** which **actual resident Qwen model, quantization, direct/serial specialist/reviewer routing** produces the best independently true final user/worker outcomes on identical authentic owner SWAAG requests after all stage tokens, GPU queue/shared VRAM, wall time and effect-verification costs; whether numeric or ordered categorical relevance outputs improve those end outcomes against separately human-adjudicated same-purpose labels; whether modern Qwen auxiliary syntax training improves such outcomes at matched compute. The current Nitro evidence set has **zero** completed model-selected source→final answer chains. No amount of additional scoring, synthetic scripts, copied journals or published-task surrogates can supply that missing observation.

**Operational decision now:** Keep a single capable initial communication call, retain original/source/time/context and role-conditioned metadata, allow independently necessary background worker calls, require actual receipt/source/output checks for consequential results, and avoid automatic reviewer escalation based on agreement alone. Do **not** deploy a general fact verifier from the exact-heading controls or change Qwen routing/weights/syntax training from these data. To revisit architecture as a scientific result later requires an *authorized resource/admission window* and pre-frozen independently graded real SWAAG case set with **both** actual completed direct and complete model-selected answer/reviewer outputs, all token/GPU/latency costs and positive/negative controls. The previously denied Thor batch/data movement **remains denied** and is not part of this plan unless independently and explicitly reauthorized later. No new calendar event was created.
