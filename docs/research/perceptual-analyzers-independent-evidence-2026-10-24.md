# Perceptual Analyzer Research — Independent Evidence and Candidate Selection

**Date:** 2026-10-24. **Project:** SWAAG visual testing / perception research. **Decision:** no image-only universal GUI/diagram/industrial/3D/video defect gate is verified; use narrowly proven instruments, reject poorly calibrated judges, and reserve human adjudication for physical realism and content correctness. This new work does not close the original task. Raw public third-party test images remain outside Git under `/data/var/perceptual-independent-20261024/`; SHA-256 receipts, scripts and aggregate results are reproducible. Sources and licensing apply individually.

## Independent local evaluations: not cherry-picked toy examples

### Dataset selection, independently established ground truth

- UIJudgeBench v0.4.0, original audited benchmark: https://github.com/gojiplus/uijudge-bench ; pinned dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/gojiberries/uijudge-bench/tree/v0.4.0 . Authors publish 3,830 provenance-tracked items across 665 frozen web pages. Only **real website** tasks at L1 were selected with a prespecified filter: layout and WCAG 1.4.3 text contrast, both original `dev` and `test` splits. This produced **89 independently labeled full-page criterion decisions on 70 real-website screenshot assets** (69 unique pixel hashes). **These full-page criteria are not guaranteed visible inside the snapshot.** Layout: 26 dev decisions over 25 pages and six test decisions over six pages, split clean vs violations evenly. Contrast: 41 dev decisions over 39 pages (4 violation, 37 compliant), 16 test decisions over 15 pages (2 violation, 14 compliant). Test and dev pages do not overlap within either task. Dataset labels use actual browser measurements and deliberate mutations of real sites. These are externally labelled **real-site renderings**, not independently recruited human judgments of naturally occurring layout defects. Some pages contribute more than one item; do not treat 89 decisions as 89 independent sites or report confidence intervals assuming independent events. Specific criterion labels are used only **after** pixel-based predictions are stored.
- Public original-size PNGs for every page and each SHA-256 are retained in `/data/var/perceptual-independent-20261024/uijudge-v0.4.0/` with `real_ui_frozen_manifest.json`, original full `items.jsonl` and raw `tesseract_immutable_predictions.json`. This dataset is **not our creation** and benchmark label receipts are independent of predictions. However one pair of differently defective pages has identical PNGs, and most mutated defect anchors are outside the image area; the screenshot-only evaluation **does not satisfy matched-visual-ground-truth requirements**. Downloads and verified hashes performed exactly once per missing file, no production site browsers accessed.
- Real industrial images: https://www.mvtec.com/research-teaching/datasets/mvtec-loco-ad ; official paper Bergmann et al., *Beyond Dents and Scratches*, IJCV 2022, DOI 10.1007/s11263-022-01578-9. A public miniature mirror https://huggingface.co/datasets/saiphyohein/spec2inspect-images contains 18 original-color test photographs of **one real pushpin organizer object type** with external labels (8 good, 10 structural/logical anomalies), plus recolored variants **not** evaluated. The 18 JPEGs were downloaded and their hashes recorded under `/data/var/perceptual-independent-20261024/mvtec-loco-subset`; third-party images must **not** be committed. MVTec license **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, noncommercial research only. Their structural/logic flaws are real photographed product inspection conditions, **not independently labeled errors in rendered forklifts or warehouse machines**. Full MVTec LOCO dataset is 3,644 images across five categories; tested subset is not representative of all categories.

### GUI screenshot-only pixel/OCR baseline: actual confusion and cost

Implementation: `tests/research/public_ui_frozen_snapshot.py` (public dataset acquisition), `public_ui_ocr_benchmark.py` (predictions), with **Tesseract 5.3.4**, page segmentation 11, and per-image grayscale/contrast ROI from **rendered PNG pixels only**, *no* hidden DOM, CSS, gold receipt, or page name in the detector. Fixed pre-score rule: flag low-confidence median OCR text under 65 percent or *any* recognized word rectangle below estimated WCAG-like **4.5:1** contrast; for the contrast question use local contrast only. These are *heuristics*, not standards-certified WCAG contrast measurements: percentile foreground/background estimates inside OCR rectangles are different from actual computed foreground and background colors, and OCR drops unseen text. Thresholds fixed **before scoring** and **not tuned after results**. Processes use single-core/nice bounded CPUs, PNGs unmodified. Observed 70-page wall time about **309 seconds**, 69 completed, one timeout. Detailed original predictions + SHA + wall timing persisted; one timeout remains explicit abstention, not a pass.

| Externally labeled split/task | Violations found (TP) | False alarms (FP) | Clean accepted (TN) | Missed violations (FN) | Abstained | Precision | Recall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dev real-site layout, 26 items | 7 | 6 | 7 | 6 | 0 | 53.8% | 53.8% |
| Test real-site layout, 6 items | 3 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 50.0% | 100% |
| Dev real-site contrast, 41 items | 2 | 23 | 14 | 2 | 0 | **8.0%** | 50.0% |
| Test real-site contrast, 16 items | 2 | 10 | 3 | 0 | 1 | **16.7%** | 100% |

The apparent perfect heldout recall **does not rescue** a judge that rejects all six layout cases including three known-clean controls. **CRITICAL INPUT-MODALITY LIMITATION: this benchmark is NOT a valid test of strictly screenshot-visible page defects as sampled here. The real-site criterion receipt may refer to an element below the provided fixed screenshot, so the ground truth is not recoverable from pixels alone.** The following visibility audit is necessary to interpret every reported false negative: On the contrast test split, ten of fourteen confirmed compliant decisions were rejected and one could not be decided. The case count for contrast true violations is only two in the heldout split; uncertainty is extreme. Pooling all 89 labels for an impressive score would hide task differences and page correlations. There is no meaningful evidence to certify this simple image-only contrast method for UI acceptance. An independent audit of the full v0.4.0 `receipt.bbox` against each actual screenshot's pixel dimensions found **12 of 16 known defective layout elements entirely outside the PNG** (10/13 dev, 2/3 test); only **four** defect anchors even partially intersect the captured image. Example anchors at vertical coordinate 2015, 4009, and 6835 pixels cannot possibly be seen in a 1600-pixel-tall PNG. Moreover, two different defect-labelled page IDs (`real-irs-newsroom--clip-overflow`, `real-irs-newsroom--protrude-viewport`) share **byte-identical screenshot pixels** despite different browser-receipt defect types. This is *not* gold-label fraud: the benchmark truth is about its saved **full DOM/page**, not necessarily about a defect captured in a fixed screenshot. It is a **benchmark modality mismatch** when used as an image-only evaluation. The numerical scores above are preserved as audit/reproduction of the flawed experiment, **not** valid estimates of screenshot-only detector accuracy on visible defects. They demonstrate the infeasibility of answering all full-page criteria from cropped screenshots and the baseline's high rate of false alarms; they **cannot** fairly assign its offscreen false negatives to perception failures. To correct future experiments, render full page, scroll to the receipt's element anchor or crop the real target with surrounding context *without showing the predictor the gold label*. Accessibility and layout tests should also retain source data for semantic checks. For contrast, the majority of 57 labels derive from full-page axe reports with no localized bbox; of the five with bbox, **three** are offscreen. Their standalone screenshot compatibility likewise must be evaluated instead of assumed.

Tesseract's OCR itself can still serve as an evidence collector against **a separate intended text contract** and expected visibility/ellipsis policy; OCR confidence alone is a bad verdict.

Official comparable deterministic benchmark references: UIJudgeBench publishes `LayoutLens` layout L1 F1 0.800 and balanced accuracy 0.792 on its full v0.4.0 layout corpus (author-reported, **not reproduced on this lab**), and `axe` L1 accessibility F1 0.391 balanced accuracy 0.615; all measured on specific benchmark tasks. This supports browser-measured geometry/actual CSS/accessibility analyzers as a more defensible starting point than a screenshot-only OCR confidence gate, but it does **not** certify every visual property. The upstream benchmark notes only **2 of 86** WCAG 2.2 success criteria meet its strict complete-coverage definition; design-pair human annotations not completed. Do not present any of these general WCAG-compliance scores as complete accessibility certification.

### Industrial real-image anomaly baseline, frozen zero-shot CLIP

Reused installed local `openai/clip-vit-base-patch32` (PyTorch fp32 **CPU**, preexisting checkpoint; no GPU model loading). Inference on all 18 original photographs with a fixed pair of natural-language descriptions (normal yellow compartmented pushpin organizer vs abnormal missing/misplaced components). Threshold fixed at anomaly softmax probability 0.5 before looking at labels. It found **2/10 anomalies**, rejected **3/8 genuine good** photos; TP 2, FP 3, FN 8, TN 5, precision **40%**, recall **20%**, AUROC **0.4625**. Total wall time ~**18 seconds** including model load. The AUROC below 0.5 on only 18 observations is not a statistically meaningful claim that CLIP is universally worse than chance; it means this frozen prompt configuration **does not discriminate this industrial anomaly subset**.

Independent second one-class rule: first four normal pictures chosen from filenames as calibration examples, last four normal plus all ten anomalies held out; normalized CLIP features compared to nearest normal exemplar, with anomaly threshold fixed by *calibration normals only*. On fourteen held-out photos TP 3, FP 1, FN 7, TN 3; precision **75%**, recall **30%**, AUROC **0.65**. Four calibration images are grossly insufficient to establish robust product-normal distribution. Neither method is an acceptable mechanical anomaly judge or test of CAD physical plausibility. There was **no training on labeled defects**, nor threshold retuning to improve heldout results. Reproduction `tests/research/public_industrial_snapshot.py` and `public_industrial_clip_baseline.py`, raw immutable results outside Git.

An alternative purpose-trained industrial anomaly design is PatchCore / EfficientAD implemented in Anomalib, see https://anomalib.readthedocs.io/en/latest/markdown/guides/reference/models/image/patchcore.html : memory-bank patch features from *multiple normal images of the target object* are compared to test images; spatial anomaly heatmap can locate defects. These **require per-domain normal exemplars, training/selection, independent heldout labels**, and have **not** been evaluated on this lab for warehouse renders. Do not label PatchCore a proven winner on the user's machinery.

### Additional CPU OCR engine feasibility and negative evidence

Installed RapidOCR ONNX 1.2.3 separately under `/data/var/perceptual-independent-20261024/ocr-compat-venv`, pinning NumPy 1.26.4 and ONNXRuntime 1.17.0 because shared Nitro NumPy 2.2.6 is binary-incompatible with the existing old runtime. Shared Python/production environment was unchanged. The first RapidOCR invocation with wrong kwargs errored **before inference**, corrected to default constructor. The subsequent exact predeclared test is **not yet completed**: full page processing required tens of seconds per page under shared CPU load; partial raw per-page results are persisted in `rapidocr_immutable_predictions.json`. It must **not** be claimed to have a score, and should be judged against the same retained labels only after all cases complete. An attempt to terminate only the owned CPU benchmark process was explicitly **denied** by platform controls; no second cancellation or disguised workaround was attempted. Running-state checks do not prove the process has stopped. No Thor model call, production process stop, or other resource-contention work was authorized.

## Independent peer-reviewed evidence that narrows candidate selection

1. **GUI quality/reasoning:** Mao et al., *Reasoning for Mobile User Experience with Multimodal LLMs: Task, Benchmark, and Approach*, CVPR Findings 2026, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026F/html/Mao_Reasoning_for_Mobile_User_Experience_with_Multimodal_LLMs_Task_Benchmark_CVPRF_2026_paper.html . UXBench has 2,000 VQA examples on actual mobile interfaces, eight UX diagnosis tasks, expert-reviewed label process. Their UI-UX 4B based on Qwen3-VL-4B-Thinking achieved **79.63%** vs Claude 4.5 Sonnet **65.50%**. These are **author-reported** task classification results, not on our screenshots, not a measurement of precision on clipping, and 20.37% errors preclude a sole acceptance gate. Official project https://github.com/afx-team/UI-UX and checkpoint https://huggingface.co/afx-team/UI-UX ; verify release checkpoint **uses updated Qwen3.5-4B base**, so the paper's Qwen3-VL-derived 79.63% cannot simply be attributed to the newer downloaded artifact. Code repository MIT; model-specific legal terms need verification before commercial use. **Candidate, untested locally.**
2. **Fine UI element localization/OCR:** Gurbuz et al., *ScreenParse: Moving Beyond Sparse Grounding with Complete Screen Parsing Supervision*, ICML PMLR 306 (2026), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/gurbuz26a.html , ScreenVLM original architecture **316 million parameters**, 55 UI element types, dense boxes and text, paper original trained 771K pages; paper reports PageIoU **0.592 vs 0.294** vanilla larger VLM on its test. Official `docling-project/ScreenVLM` model card https://huggingface.co/docling-project/ScreenVLM says public main uses newer v2 trained on **1,447,100** pages, current 746MB safetensors, **Apache-2.0**. This is a **screen parser**, not a validated general-purpose defect judge. A 55-type element parser combined with OCR/DOM geometry is a practical candidate for screenshot-only approximate bounds; test it on external defect labels. Earlier side reports using 0.606 PageIoU refer to v2 evaluation and should not be conflated with original 0.592 paper measurement. **Candidate, untested locally.**
3. **Chart and diagram semantics:** Wang et al., *CharXiv: Charting Gaps in Realistic Chart Understanding in Multimodal LLMs*, NeurIPS 2024, https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/cdf6f8e9fd9aeaf79b6024caec24f15b-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html . **2,323 real expert-reviewed scientific charts**, at publication GPT-4o 47.1% reasoning accuracy vs 80.5% human, showing older general VLMs badly misunderstand even visible diagrams. These **2024 model results cannot rank 2026 models**. For exact generated diagrams, structural semantic source of truth—labels, edges, topology, chart data, scales—must be validated *against actual pixels*, and visual-overlap/legibility separately checked; this does not certify mathematical/semantic comprehension. **No independent rendered-diagram benchmark run this pass**; prior homemade wire examples remain inadequate.
4. **Fine-grained VLM limits:** Yu et al. FG-BMK 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19053 , 1.01M image-detail questions across 280k images, reports deficient fine-grained recognition, grounding and category knowledge in general VLMs. This limits the plausibility of delegating mechanical part-count, interpenetration or fine text quality to a single caption model. **Research-supported limitation, no claim of direct machinery accuracy.**
5. **Machine/photos and geometry:** MVTec LOCO official dataset cited above has 3,644 real industrial images with logical and structural pixel-level labels (academic/noncommercial). A photo anomaly detector's trained normal-distribution deviation is not equal to **physical possibility in a rendered 3D simulation**. For owned CAD/Blender/GLB objects, deterministic tests with authoritative scene geometry, unit conventions, contact/intersection/broadphase collision, UV/normals, constraints, motion/joints and camera transformations can *prove narrow numeric properties*. They cannot by themselves judge realistic lighting, texture, design authenticity, unseen surfaces or user-perceived plausibility. A multidisciplinary benchmark such as PVBench *Quantifying Physical Plausibility in Video Generation with Object-Level Physical Measurements*, arXiv 2610.26711, 2026-10-19, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26711, proposes 46 physical measures, 600 prompts and object-level pose/scale/kinematics; **too recent/unreplicated here** and focused on generative video, not a proved forklift validator.
6. **Video / rendering / temporal continuity:** IntelLabs CGVQM+D (Jindal et al., *Computer Graphics Forum*, 2025 DOI 10.1111/cgf.70221) primary project https://github.com/IntelLabs/cgvqm and dataset https://github.com/IntelLabs/cgvqm/releases : learned **full-reference** rendered-video quality metric, comparing a reference with impaired videos for spatial and temporal artifacts, author data about 75 video scenes/pairs with human DMOS. Apache-2.0 code; dataset separately licensed (check before use). **Cannot run on a single user video without a good matched reference**; no local calibrated score performed. VBench-2.0, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21755 (2025), evaluates generative video physical fidelity and commonsense via specialist and VLM components with human evaluation, **not** frame-perfect dropped-frame/jitter verification. CSVTED inter-frame tampering dataset, original real clips with inserted/deleted/duplicated frame versions, https://huggingface.co/datasets/CardiffVisualComputing/CSVTED , provides more apt objective temporal gold, but published archives are tens of gigabytes and were **not** downloaded or benchmarked under shared-resource constraints. Existing local 180-frame real-video sample with no exact duplicates has **no independent stutter/discontinuity annotations** and is not proof of smooth video. Relevant deterministic tools: ffprobe timestamps, exact-frame hashes, shot-boundary detector, dense/sparse optical flow and trajectory tracking with explicit intentional-cut and hold annotations; no universal stutter threshold proven.

## Experimental integrity and compute

The UI evaluator used source-provided `split` and predeclared task filter; criterion-specific TP/FP/FN/TN from saved immutable pixel scores, not model output used as gold. No optimization/test threshold searching occurred; tuning a chosen classifier on public benchmark test would invalidate that test. Local industrial CLIP prompts/calibration choice frozen before external labels scored; four normal exemplar calibration set independent of fourteen-image heldout. However, sample size is very small, one object class, and upstream hosted subsets are public rather than private holdouts, with potential training contamination impossible to exclude. For formal generalization, hold out entire original sites and entire machine families, multiple fonts/themes/scales, animation types, camera cuts, small hard defects and intentional occlusions; independent humans should review ambiguous labels and generated 3D plausibility under blinded conditions. Report confidence intervals clustered by site/video/family and distinguish a “does not apply” abstention from clean.

Nitro RTX 3060 6GiB was **not borrowed for this work**. GPU free memory was around 3–4GiB at checks but production resources remain shared; Nitro and Thor service processes were not started/stopped to make room. Tesseract ran nice single-thread bounded, CLIP CPU single-thread 18 photos ~18s, public dataset retrieval 70 screenshots under ~3MB each, 18 photographs under ~600KB each; no large video dataset download or 3D rendering. The isolated RapidOCR Python interpreter did not change shared `/data/venv`. The research worktree is separated from SWAAG main and Infra dirty checkouts. An explicit platform denial on terminating an owned RapidOCR workload was respected with no retry, and no denied operation was described as successful. All user-facing result claims must distinguish completed raw observations from merely installed candidate models.
